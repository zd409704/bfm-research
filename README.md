# BFM Research

Research notes, paper analyses, and design proposals on behavior foundation models for humanoid whole-body control, including BFM-Zero, forward-backward representations, and motion tracking.

## Repository structure

- `reports/` — written analyses and design proposals
- `references/` — papers and code repositories cited by the reports
- `notes/` — reusable process notes and working notes
- `programs/life-sensorimotor/` — evolutionary sensorimotor research program and principle-track run artifacts

## Life-sensorimotor program

Research program and run artifacts for stacking life-like perception–action competence on a generic home-humanoid / plant-class platform. Stages accumulate by capability. Biology is motivation only.

- [Evolutionary research program](programs/life-sensorimotor/evolutionary-research-program.md) — S0–S7 evolutionary sensorimotor capability ladder (reflex impedance through composition and memory), with a principle, claim, and gate at each stage.
- [Principle-track run, 2026-10-22](programs/life-sensorimotor/life-sensorimotor-2026-10-22-principle/) — packets Prin-1–8 and Eng-1–2, the [program ledger](programs/life-sensorimotor/life-sensorimotor-2026-10-22-principle/program-ledger.md), and [claims](programs/life-sensorimotor/life-sensorimotor-2026-10-22-principle/claims/).
- [Follow-on run, 2026-10-23](programs/life-sensorimotor/life-sensorimotor-2026-10-23/) — adjudication, principles, and proposals for the next locked mainline packet.

Chinese adversarial reading notes that complement the reports above are in `programs/life-sensorimotor/bio-inspired/` and `programs/life-sensorimotor/bfm-zero/`.
