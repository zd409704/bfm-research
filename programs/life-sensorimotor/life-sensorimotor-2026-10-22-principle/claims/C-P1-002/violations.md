# C-P1-002 — Violations and failed transfers

- **Verified counterexample:** none established by the permitted sources; no new experiment executed. Lack of a counterexample is not a completed failed search.
- **Original wording defect:** “equivalent physical mechanism” could absorb every successful system without an absence test. The claim now names measured force histories and independently established incompatibility; if these cannot be specified, necessity use must be downgraded.
- **Passive-stability challenge:** a passive system could invalidate a claim that active feedback is universally required. The revised claim must measure passive force response or concede that the fixed-plant incompatibility premise does not apply. It cannot silently redefine the plant to survive this challenge.
- **Existing embedded feedback:** an already adequate body response defeats an inference that a new reflex module is required. It may realize the conditional role; no architectural requirement survives merely by renaming it.
- **Common safe open-loop response:** if a single admissible force history satisfies both disturbance worlds, the incompatibility premise fails. Such an example blocks applying this claim to that envelope.
- **Insufficient authority:** an infeasible envelope does not demonstrate a missing timing role. Establish feasibility separately rather than increasing rate or moving thresholds.
- **Support-mode circularity:** the decision warns that defining the envelope may depend on contact/support distinguishability. Hardware applicability remains unresolved; this record cannot require S0a research to precede every S1/S2 investigation.
- **Evidence limits:** reported scalar sweep PASS does not establish whole-body role necessity, role realization on hardware, or the absence of other implementations. A serious counterexample search with a sealed PLAN remains outstanding.

Challenges must preserve the predeclared envelope, plant, disturbance and force-accounting definitions. Log every changed premise and scope narrowing; the distinct adjudicator decides validity. Candidate status supplies no veto to pause.
