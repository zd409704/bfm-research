2. **Name the closing sequence explicitly:** reconcile record and narrowing count; verify the final statement; assemble the scope-limited package; human signature; non-author adjudicator records the transition. Signature is never sufficient alone.

4. **Pick a verification route with a stated basis.** State the cost of each allowed route as unestimated until infrastructure and reviewer availability are checked. A published-result match must cover the whole conclusion (common-input premise, paired disturbance construction, triangle bound, horizon, quantifiers), not only the solution identity. 8. **Split P's priority 2** into ranked sub-deliverables with their own cross-family adjudicator bindings and a minimum deliverable that can finish before the deadline (information-access audit first). Add the C-Eng2-A-01 claim adjudication from Q as a sub-item, explicitly not a gate.

- **Single pass.** This is PASS2 only (P = the second-drafted proposal, Q = the first-drafted proposal, per the swap). I did not read PASS1 or compare orderings, so order-swap consistency is not established here.
- **Identity leakage.** The proposal and review bodies carry author-side labels and family descriptors, and reviews cite file paths that reveal which author wrote which text. Anonymization of P and Q is nominal.
- **Reviewer family differs by side.** The two reviews come from different families, with different severity. The Q-side review was stricter on wording and costs; the P-side review was more generous on calibration. Some of the 3-point gap may reflect reviewer severity, not proposal quality. I re-scored independently, and the gap persisted (43 vs 40) but is small.
- **Source overlap.** The reviewers read extra records (claim text, Prin-1 row, roster) that the packet brief does not list; the Q-side review did not. Findings from the extra records (the provenance mismatch, the Prin-1 batching request) are unverified by me.
- **Self-disclosed coverage.** Q states it did not read upgrade or violation logs; P states it did not inspect claim bodies beyond two. Both conclusions about retirement are therefore evidence-limited.
- **Length and structure.** Both texts are about 12 KB with similar table structure; no length bias is evident.
- **Adjudicator limits.** I did not read claim records, so I cannot confirm the record-level assertions in either proposal.
