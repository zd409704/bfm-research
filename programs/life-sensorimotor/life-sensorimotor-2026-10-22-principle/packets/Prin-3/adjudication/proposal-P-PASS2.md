# Prin-3 — Competing proposal B

**LABEL=B. Author role only. Disposition proposed for adjudication; no claim, grade, decision or ledger is changed.**

## Thesis

**Recommend a conditional sequence: fix the task/channel contract, then run one discriminating amended-A packet if it is eligible; elevate amended B only for an independently specified envelope question.** The informative result of Eng-1 is a mismatch between the tested channel/detector and its placeholder acceptance target, with a strong placement dependence. It is not evidence that deeper S0a work will repair that mismatch. Automatically routing every A failure to B would substitute an engineering scheduling rule for a diagnosis.

The next experiment should distinguish a deficient detector from a channel limitation before committing to a larger envelope exercise. This is a bounded engineering investigation, not a new necessity ordering. An envelope packet can proceed first if its own task and premise checks identify it as the relevant bottleneck. If neither proposal has a task-grounded acceptance contract and a discriminating test, recommend neither for execution yet.

**Principle stance:** retain C-P1-012 as Grade-2 Candidate pending adjudication; withhold any claim that the primary DOWN channel has demonstrated the obligatory role. Retain the original all-class counterexample targets. Do not rescue necessity by deleting difficult classes, importing UP results, or turning absence of a factorial counterexample into proof.

The rate experiment also changes observer bandwidth through fixed gain-times-period tuning. Factor R therefore remains entangled with the detector implementation. The supplied summaries mention a sensitivity artifact but do not provide its contents; this proposal asserts no conclusion from it.

The factorial and substitution tests answer different questions. The former requires matching across the registered classes; the latter asks whether a particular channel realizes a proposed role under an intervention. Failing the substitution test and failing to find a factorial counterexample can coexist. Neither result cancels the other. The DOWN reference itself is weak on many classes, so non-matching against it is especially weak evidence of obligatory competence.

The one-sided certificate in C-Eng1-A-01 provides no converse: failure to establish a lower floor is not an upper bound on available information. The oracle's stronger performance is a fixture check, not an implementable independent contact channel or proof that a permitted detector can match it.

## Eng-2 recommendation: conditional sequence, gates before ranking

### Eligibility gates

1. **Principle and governance.** Check actual Locked constraints before ranking. None of C-P1-012, C-P1-002, C-P1-011 or C-Eng1-A-01 may be elevated from Candidate to a veto by this packet. No Locked Grade-1 without independent verification and human signature. D-Prin-2-001 means no ladder edge is an Eng-1/Eng-2 necessity gate. D-P1-001 remains a Provisional acceptance-gate recommendation, not a necessity order.
2. **Task contract.** Name a home-humanoid / target-plant task owner, task classes, support mode, disturbance/resource scope, q, false-event units and finite latency deadline. Obtain the outstanding human ratification before claiming task acceptance. Placeholder targets may support an explicitly labeled exploratory packet, but cannot settle target-task sufficiency or necessity. Do not infer a task from the successful d50 class.
3. **Channel contract.** Specify the physical sensor/nuisance placement and permitted joint channel `(X,W)`, including commands and any memory. Keep DOWN and UP separate. Demonstrate that the proposed target realization matches the chosen scope; otherwise declare a new engineering scope, without transferring claim support.
4. **Experiment validity.** Seal a new PLAN, calibration/evaluation split, uncertainty and multiplicity policy, failure/miss treatment, stop rule and bypassed-edge list with grades. Maintain non-interference. Separate harness validity, task sufficiency, factorial matching and role realization in the verdict. Reuse the filed FAIL as evidence, never as a tuning/evaluation split for a claimed new confirmatory result.
5. **Branch-specific eligibility.** Amended A needs a credible permitted detector change and a comparison that can discriminate detector inadequacy from observed export inadequacy. Amended B needs a fixed feasible envelope, coupled target modes and a question the prior scalar exercise did not answer. Its frozen low-information spike remains ineligible, as Eng-1 already required amendment before elevation.

These are proposed research and acceptance gates, not newly Locked principles. Task-specific safety acceptance can constrain integration without forbidding independent research.

### Recommend: contract first, then one bounded amended-A discriminator

After eligibility, rank amended A first for its direct connection to the observed uncertainty and its reversible cost. Hold the in-scope channel and task fixed. Compare the existing observer against one predeclared alternative detector using only the same allowed information. Use equal calibration budgets, fresh evaluation data and the original factorial alternatives. For the rate comparison, predeclare a bandwidth-matched control in addition to per-rate tuning, and state what aliasing or temporal information each channel loses. UP can serve only as a separately labeled placement comparator.

The output is a class-by-class task result and a renewed counterexample/realization report, not a compulsory new export schema. Bound this to one resealed packet; do not search thresholds or class subsets until it passes.

| Amended-A outcome | Next disposition |
|---|---|
| A permitted detector meets the ratified task on DOWN | Consider limited engineering acceptance for those predeclared classes, subject to latency and coverage; no necessity upgrade |
| A command-only or slower alternative meets the task and registered matching rule | File the applicable Factor-E/R counterexample for claim adjudication; rank the simpler adequate implementation |
| Both tested detectors miss while harness checks pass | Close the bounded test with insufficiency of tested implementations; no channel-impossibility claim. Reconsider task/channel design or neither |
| Task review independently identifies an unresolved finite-horizon body-envelope bottleneck | Advance amended B, without claiming that A's FAIL proved this bottleneck |

### Alternate: amended B when the envelope question is independently justified

B is appropriate when a task-relevant finite-horizon envelope has an unresolved deadline, authority or support-mode question. Its amended PLAN should test coupled modes, passive/embedded responses and disturbances relevant to that envelope, rather than repeat the scalar harness. Include a serious search for a safe common corrective history if the packet claims evidence about C-P1-002. A failure to find one is not a verified incompatible-history premise; that requires independent dynamics/reachability support and the pending information/common-prefix conditions.

An envelope result can quantify a required response deadline or expose infeasible authority. It cannot by itself certify contact knowledge, require a particular effort export, or repair C-P1-012. If B becomes eligible before amended A, it may run first as a planning choice. Neither branch is universally first.

**Reject:** automatic unamended B; an A retry that merely loosens thresholds, selects the winning class or silently imports UP; any recommendation to treat the filed FAIL as a claim downgrade. **Neither** is the execution recommendation when no branch passes its applicable gates.

## Risk of over-reading FAIL

The principal risk is turning three different negatives—A02 failure, failure to find the registered factorial counterexample, and failure of DOWN role realization—into a single verdict about necessity. That would either over-promote the claim because alternatives lost, or over-retire it because the selected detector lost. Preserve separate outcome fields, retain unfavorable classes and margins, and count any substantive scope rescue. All proposed decisions remain subject to independent adjudication.
