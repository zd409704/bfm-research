# Draft A summary — Prin-6 (C-P1-012 evidence-to-action table)

**Author:** A (Claude Opus). Full proposal: `../../proposals/A.md`.

## Thesis (one paragraph)
Future C-P1-012 evidence should reach the record through a table, fixed in advance, that gives the **strongest action allowed** for each evidence class and quality tier. The adjudicator may always choose less. The tier is assigned **before** the result is read: T0 inadmissible, T1 harness-valid but competence-unmet, T2 competent and prospective, T3 target-grounded. Favourable results can at most be **annotated** as sufficiency or realization. Adverse, competent, in-premise results can reach **claim-adj review**. Every rescue or narrowing is an **amend via B.9.6** and is counted individually (current count 1; a third accepted event triggers retirement review). *Unresolved* comparisons are annotate-only.

## Non-claims (short)
Grade stays 2 Candidate. No grade movement from any row. No lock, recommended-for-lock or retire decision here. Eng-1 FAIL is not necessity evidence. No export field, sensor type, numeric rate or research order is implied. No Eng-2 gate change. No PLAN sealed, and no new thresholds or margins set. UP results are out of scope. Unlisted evidence defaults to annotate. No named products or companies.

## Open weaknesses
Tier gaming through loose CI families (mitigation: adjudicator demotion). The repeated-S-2 trigger is heuristic. The F-2 boundary is a judgment call. The table needs re-adjudication if the claim is ever Locked.
