# Prin-6 — Proposal B: Require a concrete witness for each inference

**LABEL=B. Recommendation:** adopt the prospective evidence-to-action table below. **C-P1-012 remains Grade 2, Candidate.** This proposal changes no claim record, runs no spike, seals no PLAN, and changes no Eng-2 gate.

## Thesis

The useful unit of evidence is a **competent system under declared premises**, not a favorable flag. A failed detector cannot establish that its input channel is necessary; a successful substitution can establish dependence of a particular detector without establishing necessity across detectors. Conversely, one competent, premise-matched alternative deserves review even if a preferred reference or a second diagnostic design performs poorly.

This yields an asymmetric policy: positive counterexamples can defeat a universal claim, while unsuccessful searches only delimit what was tried. It also prevents a composition error specific to this claim. Commanded-at-R1 can challenge Factor E while realized-at-R2 challenges Factor R; those two systems do not demonstrate that commanded-at-R2 works. Separate factor falsifications warrant the existing retirement-nomination review, but must not be described as one demonstrated system lacking both properties or as proof that every effort-information role is dispensable.

## Prospective evidence record

Each future submission should attach the exact PLAN and artifacts, system identity, task contract, contact classes, and the following distinctions. They govern what can be inferred from evidence; they do not create Eng-2 execution preconditions.

- **Premise status:** matched, failed, or unresolved. Declare measurement point, nuisance placement and residual unmodeled effects, permitted joint signals and histories, calibration provenance, and independent contact channels. A signal's name does not establish its information content. UP remains excluded. An unresolved access audit cannot be silently recorded as absence.
- **Absolute competence:** met, failed, or unresolved against the same declared task's detection, fixed false-event, and latency requirements. Report each registered class; retain every `d ≥ 10 ms` class for the factorial falsifier. A02-class results must identify the actual acceptance contract; this packet does not reconstruct its missing thresholds or equate a harness acceptance flag with target competence.
- **Factor comparison:** competent match, established failure of the registered matching rule, or unresolved. Preserve `ΔTPR ≤ 0.05`, `Δp95 ≤ 10 ms`, and p95 itself. Seal comparison direction, miss handling, event accounting, confidence family and uncertainty decision rules prospectively. A point estimate inside a margin is not automatically an established match; infinity minus infinity is never a finite latency match. Do not replace p95 with a median or restricted mean.
- **Intervention result:** met, unmet, or unresolved under an explicitly preregistered substitution margin and calibration policy. Keep this separate from factorial matching. The filed cutoff is not supplied in these sources and is not inferred from observed drops. Preserve the filed DOWN criterion and d50_M10; a future revised criterion must be identified as a revision, never used to erase that outcome.

Fair per-rate tuning remains required by H2. Additional bandwidth controls can diagnose a contrast but cannot become a two-design admission condition for an otherwise competent, in-premise counterexample. The all-detector scope remains; one observer family's failures cannot restrict it.

## Applying overlapping rows

Keep the evidence components separate instead of selecting the most favorable row. For example, competent DOWN plus a successful substitution and a competent E counterexample supports realization in the first detector and simultaneously challenges necessity of its effort source. There is no contradiction: realization and obligation are different propositions. An acceptance failure likewise does not cancel a valid counterexample from another independently competent system.

Submit one review bundle identifying the exact affected factor, scope, artifacts and proposed disposition. The runner files results under B.9.8; the bound non-author claim adjudicator decides. New annotations must not disguise premise changes. Proposed narrowing event numbers remain provisional until adjudicated; current count 1 alone does not trigger retirement review.
