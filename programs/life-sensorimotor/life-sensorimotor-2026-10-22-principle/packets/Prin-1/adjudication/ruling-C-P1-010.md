## Justification

1. Accept the proposed restriction to the stated linear, single-degree-of-freedom sampled impedance class; record this as B.9.6 narrowing event #1.
2. The supplied record asserts an iff result but supplies neither its proof nor an independently checked premise match.
3. With a strict inequality, the stated K_max is a supremum, not an attained maximum; equality must be resolved explicitly.
4. Device-port passivity and stability or passivity of one particular coupled environment are different tests.
5. Part B must inherit all Part A assumptions and restrict its alternatives to the covered controller class; broader architectures are not excluded.
6. No registered violation is admitted, and an unexecuted sanity check supplies no verification.
7. D-P1-001 remains an acceptance-gate decision, not a necessity ordering.
