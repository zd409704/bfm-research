## Justification (≤12 lines)
1. Conditional kernel (paired worlds indistinguishable to force channels through τ*, no common safe prefix → world-dependent response needed before τ*) is implementation-independent and survives ablation.
2. Two bridges are open, and either one blocks the edge: (a) Q1 and the incompatible-prefix premise are not established for the target plant; (b) the required response may be realized inside S1, passively or by embedded feedback.
3. Labelling every such response "S0a" would be a post-hoc redefinition (relabelling), not a stage prerequisite. I agree with the author.
4. At most, the kernel supports a timely-response *role* candidate (cf. C-P1-002). It does not support an S0a-before-S1 *edge*. Runtime availability ≠ acquisition order.
5. Retained as the most promising Grade-2 route among the even edges. It should be pursued as a role claim, not as this edge.
6. Next: specify Q1, τ*, tolerances; derive the prefix deadline from dynamics; seal a PLAN covering passive-only, embedded and joint-controller candidates.
