# Baseline 02: How the Joint FB + Task-Q + Planner Paradigm Surpasses Imitation Data and Drives Exploration

## 1. Core Mechanism: Demonstrations as Exploration Anchors Rather Than Optimal Policies

In the joint **FB + Low-Level Actor + Task Critic $Q$ + High-Level Planner** architecture trained from random initialization, demonstration trajectories serve exclusively to initialize and guide exploration in the latent space $z = B(m)$, rather than acting as supervisory action labels or per-frame tracking targets.

The system breaks through the state-action support of the demonstration library via five coupled mechanisms:
1. **No Supervised Action Regression**: The low-level actor $\pi_\theta(o, z)$ is trained by maximizing the successor inner product $F(o, \pi_\theta(o, z), z)^\top z$, not by minimizing $\|a - a_{\mathrm{demo}}\|^2$. Even when conditioned on an expert-encoded latent $z_{\mathrm{exp}} = B(m_{\mathrm{demo}})$, the actor optimizes discounted future reachability of $B$-features under real physics rather than copying single-step joint commands.
2. **Continuous Latent Perturbation (`near-z` and Random Sphere)**: Rollouts inject local tangent-space perturbations $z_{\mathrm{near}}$ ($10^\circ$–$20^\circ$ around $z_{\mathrm{exp}}$) and planner-proposed $z_{\mathrm{plan}}$, while off-policy FB updates train on a 50/50 mixture of permuted replay goal latents and uniform spherical latents $\mathcal{S}^{d_z-1}(\sqrt{d_z})$. This populates a continuous behavioral manifold around and beyond the demonstration frames.
3. **Unindexed Single-Frame Backward Encoding**: Because $B(m)$ encodes a single frame without demonstration timestamps or phase locks, the high-level planner $P_\psi$ can re-order, hold, skip, or stitch latent skills in response to real-time observations.
4. **Independent Task Critic $Q$**: The task critic $Q_\phi$ trains via temporal-difference learning on **actual task rewards and real environment transitions** (whether generated by `expert`, `near`, or `planner` latents). Whenever an off-support perturbation or recovery maneuver achieves higher task return than the nominal demonstration, $Q_\phi$ assigns it higher value and shifts the planner $P_\psi$ toward that superior behavior.

## 2. Paradigm Comparison Across Four Learning Frameworks

| Dimension | Behavioral Cloning (BC) | Reference Tracking RL (DeepMimic-Style) | Imitation Prior + Task RL (AMP / Pretrained Skill Space) | Joint FB + Task-Q + Planner (This Paradigm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Role of Demonstrations** | Direct supervised target $s \mapsto a_{\mathrm{demo}}$ | Per-frame pose tracking reward $r_{\mathrm{track}}(s_t, m_t^{\*}) + r_{\mathrm{task}}$ | Discriminator style reward or frozen pretrained low-level skill space | Unindexed single-frame latent prompts $z = B(m_{\mathrm{demo}})$ for rollout seeding only |
| **Low-Level Actor Objective** | $\min_\theta \|a - a_{\mathrm{demo}}\|^2$ | $\max_\theta J(r_{\mathrm{track}} + r_{\mathrm{task}})$ | Pretrained or style-regularized policy gradient | $\max_\theta F(o, \pi_\theta(o,z), z)^\top z$ (plus optional task-$Q$ gradient in $K_{\mathrm{hold}}=1$) |
| **Temporal / Phase Freedom** | Locked to demonstration state distribution | Locked to reference phase clock $\phi \in [0,1]$ or frame index $t$ | Free timing, but constrained by discriminator distribution or frozen skill manifold | Fully phase-free: $B(m)$ is single-frame; planner $P_\psi$ selects $z$ every $K$ control steps |
| **Behavior Outside Demo Support** | Compounding covariate shift error | Penalized by $r_{\mathrm{track}}$ whenever task requires deviating from reference | Limited by frozen skill repertoire or adversarial mode collapse | Explicitly explored via `near` / `planner` rollouts and $50\%$ spherical FB updates; evaluated by task $Q$ |
| **Primary Failure Modes** | Out-of-distribution collapse | Tradeoff conflict between tracking fidelity and task adaptation | Pretraining-to-task distribution mismatch; discriminator gaming | Multi-network non-stationarity from scratch; motion-only $B$ aliasing in contact tasks |

## 3. Three Levels of Behavioral Freedom Beyond Demonstrations

### 3.1 Action-Level Freedom (New Physical Execution for the Same Latent Target)
- $B(m)$ maps a 229-dim or 277-dim kinematic frame to a 256-dim vector $z$ on the sphere of radius $\sqrt{d_z} = 16$. This is a many-to-one kinematic projection that discards environment geometry and actuator commands.
- The FB objective $F(o, a, z)^\top B(m')$ evaluates **discounted multi-step state visitation** under real physics ($\gamma_{\mathrm{FB}}$), not single-step pose matching.
- When obstacle height, ball position, friction, or base perturbation differs from the recording environment, the low-level actor $\pi_\theta(o_{\mathrm{low}}, z)$ reads current proprioception and terrain/ball observations in $o_{\mathrm{low}}$ and synthesizes new joint torque/position targets that still drive the robot toward the future kinematic outcome represented by $z$.

### 3.2 Temporal and Sequential Freedom (Re-Ordering and Variable-Duration Execution)
- Because $m_t$ contains no frame counter or global world position $(x, y, \psi)$, any demonstration frame is a standalone directional prompt on $\mathcal{S}^{d_z-1}(\sqrt{d_z})$.
- During rollout, `CandidateProvider` samples valid demonstration frames uniformly (or by motion weights) without stage or geometry filtering, while in evaluation (`100%` planner), $P_\psi$ generates $z_t$ purely from real observation history.
- Consequently, the planner can extend preparation steps, insert an extra balance-recovery macro-step before takeoff, or transition immediately into landing stabilization—compositions that never occurred as a contiguous trajectory in the dataset.

### 3.3 Representation-Space Interpolation and Extrapolation
- **Rollout `near-z` exploration**: Tangent-space perturbations around $z_{\mathrm{exp}} = \operatorname{proj}(B(m_{\mathrm{demo}}))$ with angular offset $\theta \in [10^\circ, 20^\circ]$:
  $$
  u_{\perp} = \xi - \frac{\langle \xi, z_{\mathrm{exp}} \rangle}{\|z_{\mathrm{exp}}\|_2^2} z_{\mathrm{exp}}, \qquad z_{\mathrm{near}} = \sqrt{d_z}\bigl(\cos\theta \cdot \hat{z}_{\mathrm{exp}} + \sin\theta \cdot \hat{u}_{\perp}\bigr).
  $$
- **FB update `z_update` relabeling**: For every batch of $n$ transitions from `FBReplay`, $z_{\mathrm{update}}$ is constructed via:
  $$
  z_{\mathrm{random}} = \operatorname{proj}(\mathcal{N}(0, I_{d_z})), \quad z_{\mathrm{goal}} = \operatorname{proj}\bigl(B_{\omega}(\tilde{m}_{\mathrm{next}}[\operatorname{perm}])\bigr), \quad z_{\mathrm{update}} = \operatorname{where}(u < 0.5, z_{\mathrm{goal}}, z_{\mathrm{random}}).
  $$
- Because `FBReplay` stores real physical transitions $(o_t, a_t, o_{t+1}, m_t, m_{t+1})$ collected across all three rollout sources (`expert`, `near`, `planner`), $B_{\omega}(\tilde{m}_{\mathrm{next}})$ increasingly encodes **newly discovered physical postures** (including hand support on obstacles, stumbled recovery, and modified landing stances), expanding the grounded goal manifold beyond the initial NPZ file.

## 4. Critical Vulnerability: Motion-Only $B$ Aliasing in Contact-Rich Tasks

In both the obstacle-traversal baseline (`229`-dim $m_t$) and the ball-striking v2 baseline (`277`-dim $m_t$), the backward network $B(m)$ observes **only robot body kinematics**:
- Root linear velocity $v_H \in \mathbb{R}^3$, root angular velocity $\omega_H \in \mathbb{R}^3$, projected gravity $g \in \mathbb{R}^3$, joint positions $q \in \mathbb{R}^{29}$, joint velocities $\dot{q} \in \mathbb{R}^{29}$, and root-relative link positions and 6D rotations.
- $B(m)$ does **not** observe contact forces, obstacle height $h$, terrain scan $s_t$, or ball position/velocity.

### 4.1 What Motion-Only $B$ Can and Cannot Distinguish
- **Cannot distinguish in $B$-space**: Stepping in mid-air vs stepping onto a solid `0.40` m box with identical joint angles and root velocities; swinging a leg through empty space vs striking a ball at the exact same kinematic state. Both map to the same $e = B(m)$.
- **How the overall architecture compensates**:
  1. The forward network $F(o, a, z)$ and low-level actor $\pi_\theta(o, z)$ receive full policy observations $o$ (including terrain height scan `scan[S]` or ball state `ball_state[9]`), enabling $F$ to predict how terrain or ball geometry alters future body motion and enabling $\pi_\theta$ to adjust joint commands accordingly.
  2. The task critic $Q(H, \cdot)$ receives privileged task/geometry features (`critic_geometry` or privileged ball-interaction state) and trains directly on true task rewards (obstacle clearance + stable rear landing, or ball approach + velocity toward goal).
- **Remaining bottleneck**: If accomplishing the task requires commanding two distinct contact forces under nearly identical kinematic postures, a purely kinematic $z$ space may lack the parameterization to let the planner specify the contact force intention directly. Evaluating when motion-only $B$ suffices vs when contact-aware augmentation is required is a core open research brief (`B03`).

## 5. Non-Stationarity and Stability Risks in Joint Training from Scratch

Unlike two-stage pipelines (pretrain FB on a large mocap dataset, then freeze FB and train a high-level policy), joint training from random initialization updates six networks simultaneously:

| Risk | Mechanism of Instability | Architectural Mitigation in Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| **Drifting Semantics of $z$** | As $B_\omega$ updates, the same physical frame $m$ maps to a changing $z$, and a fixed $z$ stored in task replay changes its behavioral meaning under $\pi_\theta(o, z)$. | Never cache demonstration $z$ across updates; re-encode raw `[1, 229]` or `[1, 277]` frames online (`eval/no_grad`) at each macro boundary; use a smaller, fresher task replay window (`50k` macros) with delayed planner updates (`delay=2`). |
| **Behavior $z$ Overwrite Bug** | Replacing `behavior_z` with FB relabeled $z$ corrupts the off-policy action label read by task critic $Q$. | Strict separation: `FBReplay` stores immutable `behavior_z` while sampling temporary `z_update` inside `FBAgent.update`; `TaskReplay` stores immutable `z_actual` executed during the macro-step. |
| **Batch Normalization Shift** | Updating `obs_normalizer` / `motion_normalizer` twice on `m_next` or allowing expert queries to mutate running statistics biases $B$. | `FBAgent.update` updates BN once on real `(obs, m_current)` and once on `(next_obs, m_next)`, then freezes BN (`eval/no_grad`) for `z_update` sampling and expert encoding. |
| **Early Actor Collapse** | Before $F$ and $B$ form a coherent successor representation, maximizing $F^\top z$ pushes $\pi_\theta$ along noisy gradients, producing immediate falls or severe contact penetrations. | Orthonormalization loss $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{orth}}$ with weight $\lambda_{\mathrm{orth}}=1.0$, target EMA $\tau=0.01$ (`0.005`), pessimistic ensemble aggregation over $F$, and R2.1 separation of `severe_penetration` task failure from numerical `invalid`. |

## 6. Required Empirical Evidence to Validate "Beyond Imitation"

A valid scientific claim that this paradigm surpasses imitation data requires four quantitative evaluation tiers (none of which is satisfied by short CPU smoke runs):
1. **In-Distribution vs Out-of-Distribution Task Success**:
   - In obstacle traversal: demonstrating only $h_{\mathrm{demo}} \approx 0.704$ m, yet achieving $\ge 70\%$ 95% Wilson lower-bound success under `100%` planner control across unseen heights $h \in [0.10, 0.70]$ m and held-out OOD heights/depths.
2. **Emergence of Novel Contact Modes**:
   - Quantitative frequency and success contribution of hand-on-box support, multi-step takeoff adjustments, and stumbled landing recovery that have zero occurrence in the 859-frame source NPZ.
3. **Planner-vs-Expert Source Dominance Crossover**:
   - Longitudinal tracking of episode return $R(\text{planner})$ vs $R(\text{expert})$ and $Q_1(H, z_{\mathrm{plan}}) - Q_1(H, z_{\mathrm{exp}})$ showing the point in training where planner-selected latents outperform direct expert encodings.
4. **Compute-Matched Baseline Ablations**:
   - Direct comparison against (a) pure task PPO from scratch, (b) DeepMimic-style tracking + task reward PPO, (c) AMP + task reward PPO, and (d) joint FB without `expert`/`near` exploration prompts (`100%` planner from step 0).
