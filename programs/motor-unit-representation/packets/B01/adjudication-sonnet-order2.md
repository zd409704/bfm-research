## 4. Disposition

**Recommend: Q** (77.0 vs 71.0; margin 6.0 exceeds the 5.0 tie margin).

**Alternate: P.**

The recommendation is moderate, not decisive. Q wins on audit completeness (+5), broader mechanism coverage and richer arms (+1 on the proof row, +1 on E0–E4), and loses ground on rigor of its headline theorem. P is the more rigorous and more honest candidate, and its spike measurement design is stronger. Q is the better basis for the packet because it answers the brief's explicit deliverable (a per-group causal replacement), but it cannot be adopted as written.

### Mandatory merge items from the alternate track (P → Q)

1. **Replace Q's Theorem 2 / C-B01-Q-01 premises.** Adopt P's framing as the correct core: the `F_c` zero-residual, arbitrary-gradient family and the replay-action error `c(vᵀz)(a_b−a')` become the *non-identification* claim, while Q's zeroing result is kept as a separate claim whose premise is the full target-state sufficiency fix from §3 (and the review-B counterexample is documented as a boundary example).
2. **Adopt P's separation of causal availability from Markov sufficiency**, with fail-closed behavior: no silent truncation, and an explicit "schema binding fails" outcome if masking removes needed persistent state.
3. **Adopt P's reactive-generator rule.** The state-triggered progress counter is a separate endogenous generator state, not an element of exogenous `ξ`. It uses a reactive-generator flag. Never encode realized time-to-release. This resolves review-B's Definition 1 conflict in Q.
4. **Adopt P's world-frame schedule caveat** (a world-frame force independent of the robot still produces an action-dependent joint torque through the moment arm) into Q's Group 20 treatment.
5. **Adopt P's manifest-join record** `(group_id,start,stop,source,sample_time,action_latch_time,update_order,frame,units,normalization,class)`, and label Q's per-group widths and contents as nominal until the join exists.
6. **Adopt P's measurement design for the Jacobian test:** the Stage A analytic FP64 control; re-leafed inputs (so DETACH is measured on functional dependence); the independent branch-return reference `D_ref` with horizon and step-size convergence checks; the controllable-subset rule that depends only on physics; underpowered-test rules; and separate factual versus actor-query counterfactual panels. Replace Q's `D≥4 ⇒ χ≤0.2` gate (reversed implication) with P's direct comparison of `‖J_a‖` and `‖J_L H_a‖` against `D_ref`, and make G-1/G-3 consistent.
7. **Adopt P's pre-registered frozen seeds, no checkpoint selection, and training-failure reporting rules.**
8. **Apply the reviewer-identified arm additions to the merged protocol:** EXO-PREVIEW (endogenous slices masked, exogenous preview retained), RAW-PERMUTED (information-destroying control with matched marginals), and a fit-matched comparison. Make DETACH a bitwise-equality check, not a trained arm. Define termination and continuation-policy treatment for 64-step episodes versus long-horizon references. Use exact equality for packing checks.
9. **Fix Q's C-B01-Q-03 EMA numerics** (the 0.21 residual) and keep the paired-snapshot construction from P-02 as the operational test.
10. **Merge fix on G-0:** keep Q's BAIA, but add a provenance/read-order check and independent resampling of unrealized innovations, since action invariance alone does not establish measurability.

### Confounds

- **P/Q ordering and framing.** This is order2, and P/Q labels are swapped relative to order1. I scored without reference to order1 and cannot rule out position effects. A tie-margin-sized swing (about 5 points) across orderings would indicate a position effect, and a 6-point margin here is only barely outside that range.
- **Different problem coverage, not just quality.** Q's higher completeness and mechanism score partly reflects its willingness to supply nominal content, while P's lower score partly reflects honesty about what the frozen spec supports. If the rubric were read as penalizing unverified field content, the 5-point completeness gap would shrink and the recommendation would approach a tie. I scored Q's nominal table at a modest discount for that.
- **Neither candidate has run anything.** Both spikes are paper protocols. Q's wrong theorem and P's vacuous-leaning claim were scored on analysis alone; no empirical result adjudicates between P's non-identification and Q's zeroing mechanisms for V3.
- **Reviewer asymmetry.** The critique of Q found two CRITICAL items and the critique of P found none, but the reviewers used different severity conventions. I re-derived the main counterexample myself instead of trusting the labels. Severity labels were not used in scoring.
- **Appendix B text unavailable.** Form compliance with B.3/B.5 is judged on the candidates' self-descriptions and the reviews, so the form-compliance verdicts are provisional.
- **Shared framing risk.** Both candidates adopt the brief's causal boundary, so agreement on it is framed agreement and not independent evidence of necessity. Both candidates say this about themselves.
