## 2. Rubric-Keyed Technical Objections

**Action-Jacobian Degeneracy Proof — 35%.**

- **[CRITICAL] State recovery does not imply Theorem 2(a)'s conditional independence.** Exogeneity before conditioning does not survive conditioning on a common consequence of action and noise. Counterexample at a fixed current state: let independent Gaussian variables be action \(a\) and innovation \(w\), set \(s'=a+w\), \(\xi'=w\), and leak \(y=s'\). Choose the scalar Bellman target \(T=s'+\gamma\xi'\), realized by immediate feature \(B(g)=s'\) and a target critic returning \(\xi'\). The leak exactly reveals \(s'\), and \(w\perp a\), satisfying the stated state-revealing and noise premises. Nevertheless,
  \[
  E[T\mid y,a]=(1+\gamma)y-\gamma a,
  \qquad \partial_a F^\star=-\gamma.
  \]
  Conditional action support has interior. Thus P1–P4 do not imply a zero or unidentified derivative. The proof needs sufficiency for the entire target-generating state/history and the relevant conditional independence, not just physical \(s'\). It also drops conditioning on \(z\) in Theorem 2(b) without justification.
- **[CRITICAL] Theorem 2(d) extends an on-support statement to unsupported target-policy actions.** A correctly acknowledges arbitrary derivatives when the leak identifies the behavior action, then nevertheless asserts a unique behavior-policy fixed point whenever the leak is state-revealing everywhere. At \((p_{t+1}^{\beta},\pi_z(x_{t+1}))\), evaluation may be off replay support; arbitrary extensions can retain action and \(z\) dependence. Global action invariance and a well-defined Markov backup are additional assumptions. Neither full-measure state recovery nor replay MSE supplies them.
- **[MAJOR] Lemma 1 is a valid chain-rule identity for one fixed target, not a proof that two independently bootstrapped critics obey it.** Marginalizing a leaky target does not remove leakage in its continuation value. Likewise, the AEV residual identity requires nested information sets and the same random target; arms L/C use different bootstraps and C introduces new causal history. The claims that the marginal is the correct policy Bellman object and that their residual difference is exactly AEV are unproved. The linear-Gaussian calculation is useful under its explicit assumptions, but does not establish the general theorem.

**Completeness of 22-Group Audit — 25%.**

- **[MAJOR] The nominal classification is not an exact slice audit.** Supply the frozen manifest, offsets, timestamps, packing rules, and slot-level R/G/M decisions. G14's concatenated mean/final forces and G18's generator state need explicit width-preserving encodings. Conditional shifts of G1–5/13–17/19 also change which fields are genuinely retained; the fixed R/G dimension totals cannot certify the implementation before reconciliation.
- **[MAJOR] G-0 accepts some forbidden future information.** A post-\(t\) random disturbance draw can be action-invariant under common random numbers while not being \(\mathcal F_t^-\)-measurable. A §1.5 calls invariance the operational definition of admissibility, contradicting Definition 3. Require provenance/read-order checks and independent resampling of unrealized innovations while fixing the admissible snapshot. Finite action probes alone cannot certify every field.

**Preservation of E0–E4 Disambiguation — 20%.**

- **[MAJOR] Proposition 1 proves a conditional statement; its sufficiency premise is asserted, not demonstrated.** Enumerate the simulator's dynamic state, including actuator delay buffers, contact/friction memory, moving-obstacle generator phase, and any solver state that affects subsequent integration. Static latency/material parameters and past forces do not automatically encode these variables. Show recovery or transition-kernel equivalence before claiming exact Markov observability.
- **[MAJOR] State-triggered release requires a separate endogenous generator state.** A causal progress counter is admissible, but its update may depend on the robot and action. Putting it inside \(\xi\) conflicts with Definition 1 and G-0's requirement that \(\xi_{t+1}\) be invariant. Separate exogenous schedule parameters from current endogenous progress and specify the G20 preview for that case; a deterministic release forecast cannot be inferred from the counter alone.
- **[MAJOR] Physical state-recovery examples are unjustified.** One stop-force observation \(k_s\delta+d_s\dot\delta\) does not generally invert two unknowns, much less reconstruct the full next state. A wrench projection can be non-injective and need not reveal velocity. Establish observability with the available trajectory and known dynamics for each claimed stratum.

**Spike Falsifiability — 20%.**

- **[MAJOR] The degeneracy gate reverses a one-way implication.** Degeneracy implies large \(D\); large \(D\) does not imply degeneracy. For a scalar output, take \(J_a=1\), \(J_{\rm fut}=(M,0)\), and \(Y_a=(0,1)^\top\). Then \(D=M\), but the bypass product is zero and retention is one. C-B01-A-01's “\(D\ge4\), i.e. \(\chi\le0.2\)” is false; G-3 separately uses 0.5. Measure the actual product and direct retention against a validated causal reference. Low sign agreement alone can reflect poor fitting.
- **[MAJOR] G-4 tests a different identity from Lemma 1.** It omits the conditional expectation over innovations, uses separately trained critics, and includes only G20–21 even though A identifies leaks in G1–17. Use a common fixed target, average paired noise realizations, and include every changed action-dependent input; otherwise failure does not identify a missing pathway or nonsmoothness.

## 3. Audit of Rejected Alternative & Necessity Claim

**Rejected alternative [MAJOR].** EMA-only masking is non-trivial and worth testing; the triangle-inequality aliasing bound is sound for genuinely identical remaining inputs with different target values. Its V3 applicability is not proved. An EMA does not reach exact steady state after 25 steps: at 20 ms/step and a 320 ms time constant, roughly \(e^{-500/320}\approx0.21\) of the initial discrepancy remains. Identical recent windows also need not imply identical EMAs initialized earlier. Construct matched snapshots with identical retained observations/memory and independently varied hidden schedules. Show separation in the represented \(B\)-feature return, not only in an unrestricted successor measure. Add a stronger alternative retaining G18 generator state while simply masking future windows; the current Z arm deletes several useful variables simultaneously. Stop-gradient rejection is correct for replay leaves, but is the easier alternative.

**C-B01-A-01 [CRITICAL].** A supplies explicit premises, scope, conclusion, operational test, and falsifier, and labels the claim Candidate. Its Grade-1 necessity conclusion nevertheless fails for the reasons in §2. Architecture independence also requires realizability and differentiability; derivatives are not directly defined for arbitrary tabular critics.

**Falsifiability [MAJOR].** Held-out \(R^2\ge0.99\) is approximate predictive accuracy, not certification of exact measurability or conditional action-support interior. On singleton support, an accurate non-zero derivative is expressly allowed by non-identification, so the proposed falsifier does not refute that branch. The “within 5% of minimum” criterion lacks an independently estimable minimum. Split zeroing and non-identification into separate claims, verify their premises with analytic synthetic controls, and allow premise-satisfying counterexamples to reject the conclusion rather than automatically reclassifying them as premise failures. Appendix B compliance remains unverified without Appendix B's text.

## 4. Spike Protocol Critique

- **[MAJOR] Isolate targets and interventions.** Add common-target Monte Carlo or fixed-teacher regression controls, plus staged sanitization arms for timestamp shifts, G20, G21, and G6 masking. Specify next-state privileged inputs for counterfactual backups. Otherwise residual changes mix input causality, altered information, and different bootstrap errors. Pre-register an AEV estimator; G-6 currently contains no AEV bound despite C-B01-A-02 claiming it does.
- **[MAJOR] Make finite differences interpretable.** Specify evaluation at both behavior and actor actions, action clipping, normalization/Jacobian conversion, and a step-size sweep. Separate smooth interiors from contact switches and saturation boundaries. Bound the 150-step truncation error using the chosen discount, report rollout uncertainty, and report how many state/latent samples survive the sign-agreement filter. A finite secant across an impact is not a local derivative.
- **[MAJOR] Replace weak observability controls.** G-6's regime probe can decode the explicitly supplied scene one-hot without learning transition-relevant state. Timer permutation can create impossible timer/history combinations. Use matched, simulator-consistent schedule interventions and held-out conditional transition/return prediction across the 720 configuration/regime cells; report missing cells and strata explicitly.
- **[MAJOR] Repair thresholds and decision rules.** Near-zero factual residuals make \(R_{cf}\) unstable; report absolute common-target errors with preregistered denominator handling. Guard all gradient ratios against near-zero true slopes. Define paired seed inference and an equivalence margin for G-8 rather than “inside the seed CI.” Fixed update budgets do not certify convergence. G-2 failure with G-6 passing can still indicate hidden-state loss or estimator error; the prescribed “capacity or budget” diagnosis is not exhaustive. Preserve H1's original threshold as a separate outcome instead of interpreting a failed ratio away through probes.
