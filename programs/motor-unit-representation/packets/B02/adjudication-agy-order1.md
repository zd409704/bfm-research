### Confounds Paragraph

Three potential confounds were tracked during this adjudication and must be controlled during B02 spike execution:
1. **Framing-Induced Convergence vs Independent Necessity (Methodology §11.5)**: Because `brief.md` explicitly prescribed the `20/15/20/15/30%` E0–E4 quotas, the $\ge 25$-step E3 / $\ge 20$-step E4 windows, the `32,768` + `65,536` replay split, the inertia-scaled PD + bias + anti-windup rule structure, and the $< 0.35\text{ rad/s}$ threshold, agreement between Candidate P and Candidate Q on these elements represents framed compliance with the brief rather than independent discovery of physical necessity; only the narrowed Grade 1 claims (`C-B02-P-01`, `C-B02-P-02`, `C-B02-Q-01`, `C-B02-Q-02`) carry implementation-independent mathematical force.
2. **Evaluator-Fix vs Controller-Fix Attribution (`Issue #5` vs `Issues #1/#2/#6/#8`)**: Baseline V3's `5.89–13.88 rad/s` zero-velocity RMSE was measured on a buggy evaluation panel that injected moving targets (`Issue #5`). Consequently, a large drop in RMSE between the baseline ledger and the B02 spike will be partially driven by evaluating on a true static-holding panel rather than solely by the training fixes; the spike runner must execute a baseline-checkpoint-on-new-panel control row (`review-A-on-B` `S5`) so panel-fix gains are cleanly separated from training-fix gains.
3. **Presentation Density vs Algorithmic Soundness**: Candidate P provides more granular code-style pseudocode, explicit closed-loop eigenvalue arithmetic, and quantitative mechanism probes (`S2-9`, `C-B02-P-03`), whereas Candidate Q writes in denser prose with fewer equations; scoring strictly by verified mathematical invariants and blind-review counterexamples revealed that P's presentation concealed critical ring-overwrite (`B02-B-04`), duplicate-key (`B02-B-07`), and sign-flip underflow (`B02-B-01`) bugs, while Q's compact prose concealed critical single-sign training (`R1-a`), zero-latency E4 feedforward (`R1-b`), and global sub-case phase-locking (`R2-a`) bugs, justifying an exact `78.00` vs `78.00` tie and a mandatory cross-synthesis of both candidates.
