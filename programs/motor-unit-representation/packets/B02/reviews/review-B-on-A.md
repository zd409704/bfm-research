# Blind Review B on Proposal A (Packet B02)

Reviewed against the frozen `PROGRAM_CHARTER.md`, `packets/B02/brief.md`, and `packets/B02/proposals/A.md` only. No other review or draft was read. References below are to Proposal A's sections unless stated otherwise. Baseline layouts and the inherited B01 decision were not independently inspected; assertions about them remain conditional.

**Recommendation: major revision; do not accept the claimed readiness guarantee as specified.** The single-source scene program, physics readback, stratified replay, and separation of pipeline verification from competence are useful. However, several proposed invariants are false or mutually inconsistent before any physics experiment. A bounded storage allocation does not guarantee bounded-time closure of behavior-dependent gates.

## 2. Rubric-Keyed Technical Objections

The rubric weights below are those in the brief. Severity denotes impact on the specified contract, not an empirical claim that the eventual implementation cannot work.

### Resolution of Audit Issues #1, #2, #3, #5, #6, #8 — 35%

**B02-B-01 [CRITICAL] — The generator contradicts its own S0 predicates (#1; §§1.2–1.5, S0-1/S0-5).** The sign-flip argument proves a ≥ 0.25 S_max load only on draws that actually flip. For example, m = 0.20, σ = +1, and τ_g(q*) = −0.02 S_max satisfy F1 and produce τ_ext = −0.18 S_max. No flip occurs, so S0-5 fails. This is a counterexample within the stated parameter constraints; whether the fixed morphology set realizes it requires physics/configuration inspection. Likewise, E3 drift is constrained by the upper effort limit but lacks an explicit lower load-magnitude constraint, so it can erode the sustained-load threshold.

The declared E3 sustain window begins at p₀. For step-on, its first sample compares τ_L with zero and violates the ≤ 0.02 S_max increment predicate. Later subwindows can qualify, but the declared window does not. Define the onset edge separately and start the stable-window predicate after it. Finally, contact and escape cannot be checked on torque/program arrays at reset; S0 must distinguish schedule checks from S1 physical predicates. Specify a generator that satisfies exactly the same predicates used by the evaluator, including lower load bounds under drift.

**B02-B-02 [MAJOR] — Rule calibration is wired conceptually, but its initialization and recovery argument are wrong (#2; §§1.2, 2.1–2.2).** During locked pre-roll at q₀ = q*, with a static reference, e = 0. The stated integral update is therefore zero regardless of gravity-model error or applied load. The lock supplies the missing reaction; the integral has not “absorbed” model error as claimed. E4 training then has only 20–28 loaded control steps, κ = 0, and integral authority limited to 0.6 S_max even when the required holding effort reaches 0.85. Proportional error may supply the remainder, but neither absorbed load nor near-zero position error follows by construction. The idealized linear eigenvalue calculation does not establish the noisy, saturated, contact-bearing controller's performance across all configurations. Define controller state initialization and validate loaded settling and post-release behavior rather than assuming them. Also specify whether exploration noise is disabled for the rule positive control.

**B02-B-03 [MAJOR] — Anti-saturation is a plausible intervention, not the claimed bound (#8; §2.7, C-B02-A-02).** A finite squared hinge with λ = 0.1 does not enforce |y| ≤ 2. For a scalar normalized critic objective Q(tanh y), stationary points above 2 satisfy Q_a sech²(y) = 0.2(y − 2); the assumptions do not exclude such points. Normalizing objective magnitude does not bound its action derivative. The derivative lower bound is valid conditional on |y| ≤ 2, but the loss does not establish that condition. The §2.7 bound for the full loss also omits the objective normalization factor and cannot generally survive batch-gradient cancellation. Restrict the theorem to the head Jacobian, treat the penalty as empirical recovery assistance, and make recovery a required audit-#8 result. Feasible static effort at q* does not prove transient feasibility, or establish that shared-network learning preserves every holding fixed point.

**B02-B-04 [CRITICAL] — Slot counts do not preserve complete resident episodes during writes (#3; §§2.2, 2.6, 3.3).** In a full 68-slot policy ring, four transitions preceding the latest 64-step episode can be overwritten safely. The fifth write of the next episode evicts the previous episode's first transition, while the new episode has only five transitions. Neither episode is complete. GC-2 therefore fails during writes 5–63. A 91-slot ring similarly loses its complete episode beginning at write 28. Capacity ≥ T proves residency after a completed commit, not “always.”

The rule case is more direct: overwriting a 34-step E3 window creates fragments from different episodes, which cannot jointly establish one contiguous 25-step event. Constant occupancy is not constant qualifying coverage. Specify audit/sampling boundaries, episode identity, validation, and an atomic commit protocol. If guarantees are only at episode boundaries, say so and prohibit reads of intermediate states. Failed episodes still need a real recovery mechanism; see B02-B-09.

**B02-B-05 [MAJOR] — Normalizer output preservation is sound only under explicit architectural conditions; optimizer handling is not justified (#6; §2.5).** The affine W′, b′ formulas correctly preserve a first-layer preactivation for an unclipped affine normalizer and unchanged raw inputs. Enumerate all consumers, bypasses, clipping operations, and targets, and update them atomically. Shared statistics align preprocessing, not the underlying rule/policy distributions.

Adam moments are gradient statistics, not parameter values. Multiplying them by the same factors as W does not follow from this reparameterization; the bias shift also couples weight and bias coordinates. Even a pure scaling gives inverse scaling for gradients under the corresponding coordinate change. State whether optimizer state is reset or approximately transformed, and test subsequent updates, not merely instantaneous forward equality. The sampler's fixed 171/341 split is also not exactly 1/3 versus 2/3; use realized long-run probabilities in the “exact marginal weights” claim or rotate that rounding too.

**B02-B-06 [MAJOR] — The exact holding target remains conditional (#5; §3.1).** The proposal explicitly assumes a calibration convention outside its read set and gives two incompatible g13 layouts. Resolve the frozen manifest before registration, including units, normalized coordinates, housing-frame origin/axes, and availability of every descriptor used by FK or gravity estimation. An L13-U effort target based on gravity alone is not an equilibrium outcome under unknown E3 load; sensitivity reporting does not make it exact. The 1,000-transition FK check is useful but must cover all configurations and the actual feature encoding, not just raw position in meters. The final target construction must demonstrably use only deployment-permitted data.

### 720-Cell Cross-Coverage Guarantee — 25%

**B02-B-07 [CRITICAL] — The two E4 rule events have the same deterministic key (§§1.2, 1.4, 2.2).** P0-R requests E4-R and E4-RI at ordinal 0, but make_program takes no variant argument and n mod 3 = 0 selects R. Identical keys produce identical programs; the claimed RI episode cannot be generated as specified. Repeated keys also leave the sidecar unable to distinguish independent episode identities for deduplication. Give the two streams unique ordinals selecting the intended variants, or add a persistent variant/episode identifier to the key and memory budget. Specify how these identities advance during refresh and for reserve windows.

**B02-B-08 [CRITICAL] — GC-5 depends on policy competence; open-loop forcing cannot guarantee it (§§1.4–1.5, 3.3).** An E2 obstacle does not force the robot to approach it. The single rule contact event is insufficient for GC-5's two events if the resident policy episode stays on the free side. A moving q_ref cannot fix this for a policy receiving an unrelated sampled z. E4's no-contact recovery predicate can also fail under uncontrolled motion. Thus §3.3's statement that none of the counts depends on policy competence is false. Top-up may improve the odds but provides no finite bound, and changes the nominal episode quotas.

There are additional gate inconsistencies: GC-5 requires a rule escape in every E1 cell, effectively 144/144, while S1-5 allows only 95%. Section 2.2 requires every E3 rule anchor below 0.10 rad/s and 0.02 rad, while S1-4 permits 5% to fail those limits. Separate scheduled opportunities, physically realized events, and competence metrics. Either admit conditional closure or supply a feasible acquisition mechanism and bounded failure/retry contract. Preserve unsuccessful valid rollouts rather than silently selecting only successes.

### Memory & Compute Compliance — 20%

**B02-B-09 [CRITICAL] — Rollback has no storage for overwritten data (§§1.2, 2.2, 2.6).** A cursor restores an address, not the old FP32 transition. In-place writes destroy the data needed by the promised slot-for-slot rollback after a late NaN or solver failure. The 16 policy reserve slots cannot back up a 64-transition episode. A 64-row staging copy alone costs 2,667,328 B, exceeding the 1,855,568 B nominal tensor headroom if added without reclaiming storage. This does not prove that an atomic design is impossible; it shows that the specified zero-staging design is incomplete. Provide a concrete storage/commit layout within both partition caps and measure memory including normalizer scratch, optimizer state, evaluation, and rollback. Deterministic regeneration would require the old behavior state and physics replay, not merely the program key.

**B02-B-10 [MAJOR] — There is no bounded compute-to-closure argument (§§1.2, 1.3, 3.3, 6).** Failed-rollout retries and front-of-queue repairs can continue indefinitely. The episode-to-gradient-update ratio is unspecified, so 4,096 updates do not imply policy rings are full or all load/variant strata are refreshed. Sixty-four rule refreshes cover only 64 of 144 configurations; a full cycle takes 9,216 updates. A scheduler pointer alone does not capture per-cell ordinals, repair queues, ring cursors, behavior/exploration randomness, or in-flight histories. Freeze the complete schedule and checkpoint state, bound retries and wall time for S0–S2 as well as S3, and report unresolved cells as failure to meet the engineering budget.

### Spike Falsifiability — 20%

**B02-B-11 [MAJOR] — Some required fixes can fail without falsifying the advertised engineering contract (§6).** The concluding falsification list omits S1-4, S1-7, and S2-9, even though rule anchors, a usable static panel, and saturation recovery support the claimed resolutions. A failed rule positive control does not prove physical infeasibility: it could expose bad gains, integral initialization, noise, or a controller bug. Record CONTROL_FAIL separately from proven INFEASIBLE and mark the proposal unvalidated. An inconclusive saturation probe must leave #8 unresolved. Section 4 below details changes needed for decisive outcomes.

## 3. Audit of Rejected Alternative & Necessity Claim

**C-B02-A-01: accept the narrow full-episode counting bound; reject its use to rule out global FIFO replay in general.**

1. **Valid result.** If every one of 720 mutually exclusive cells must simultaneously retain a complete 64-transition episode in the rule partition, 720 × 64 = 46,080 > 32,768 makes that requirement impossible. This is independent of learner quality. The event bound N ≥ Σ m_s ℓ_s additionally needs distinct resident witnesses with no shared transition support between counted events; make that explicit. The proposal's once-per-episode rule can support such an assumption for the relevant same-type events.

2. **Overstated conclusion [MAJOR].** A mean retained length need only be ≤ 32,768/720 = 45.511…, not ≤ floor(32,768/720) = 45. Integer individual lengths can have a fractional mean. The floor applies to a uniform per-cell allocation. Further, the bound concerns simultaneous residency, not cumulative coverage from full physical rollouts, and does not uniquely require contiguous windows for cells whose gate is only transition occupancy.

3. **IID occupancy is not FIFO impossibility [MAJOR].** The exact expected empty-cell count for 1,024 IID policy episodes is 144 Σ_s(1 − p_s/144)^1024 ≈ 185.469; the stated ≈ 185.6 is a reasonable Poisson approximation. The bound on joint occupancy needs a justification for occupancy dependence, rather than being described solely as counting. Regardless, it applies to IID insertion, not all FIFO schedules. The proposal itself acknowledges that a deterministic schedule with maximum gap ≤ 1,024 episodes can preserve policy-cell residency at commit boundaries. A simple 720-cell cycle already disproves an unconditional FIFO impossibility claim, though that particular cycle does not satisfy the scene percentages. Any quota-preserving version must separately prove its maximum gaps. A 64-episode gap margin is not destroyed by every single retry or top-up.

4. **The rejected alternative bundles independent decisions [MAJOR].** FIFO storage does not require endogenous release, and event-triggered release does not require leaking a future realized release time into F. Under the charter, current/historical state of an endogenous release mechanism can be represented causally; falsely calling a realized future time exogenous is the problem. This review cannot verify the stronger unread B01 restriction. Compare storage policies under the same causal generator, event definitions, quotas, and transactional boundaries. The narrow full-rule-episode version is already excluded by capacity; other FIFO/window or hybrid designs are not.

5. **The objections partly apply to A itself.** A also uses coverage-driven top-up, outcome-dependent retry, and physical event flags. Its ledger is therefore not a function of the scheduler pointer alone. Discarding invalid solver output is reasonable, but its retry policy and valid failure retention must be explicit in either design.

Retain C-B02-A-01 as a conditional capacity lemma. Remove the universal “global FIFO with a hard per-cell gate” exclusion and any claim that it establishes the necessity of A's particular slot design without a matched comparison.

## 4. Spike Protocol Critique

The proposed spike has worthwhile safeguards: three Full seeds, explicit thresholds, real-physics readbacks, independent panel keys, memory measurements, and a negative control. It can become decisive after the following corrections.

1. **Freeze one executable contract before S0.** Resolve g13, E4 keys, the sustain-window boundary, drift/load bounds, variant selection, rule noise, and the exact normalizer initialization. Include collector/evaluator/manifest hashes and the config-to-capacity mapping, not just make_program and gate constants. Distinguish tests of exogenous arrays from tests requiring physical trajectories.

2. **Add adversarial replay tests before expensive physics.** Fill every cell, interleave reads with each replacement write, and inject failures immediately before and after overwritten event evidence. Recompute events using unique episode IDs and contiguous resident steps. Assert rollback restores old data byte-for-byte. Checkpoint/resume during a refresh and repair cycle, as well as at a clean update boundary. Event flags must not allow fragments of different episodes to masquerade as one witness.

3. **Make the coverage oracle independently auditable.** Save actual applied torques, contact observations, and event intervals for validation outside the training replay, with provenance and storage/RSS accounting. An event bit alone cannot independently reproduce the readback predicate. E1's no-return condition extends to t = 64, beyond its stored 34-step window; explicitly distinguish an audited whole-rollout certificate from a fully resident event witness. Include counterexamples with sufficient slot occupancy but zero qualifying events.

4. **Repair the negative-control injection.** A “40-step left shift” needs padding/truncation semantics. Edge-padding can leave an E3 plateau satisfying the label-level predicate, whereas zero-padding can force a release; exact injected-count detection is not specified until this is settled. Define whether detection checks the prescribed onset time or only regime eligibility. Use disjoint, enumerated affected episode IDs, guarantee each injection violates a named predicate, and count each mismatching episode once. Section 1.5 currently alternates between episodes and counts per event type. Also include untouched controls to measure false positives.

5. **Reconcile every S1/S2 threshold.** Use one rule-anchor and escape standard across §2.2, GC-1–GC-6, and S1. Explain how reserve windows affect S1-2's “exactly 144” E3 events: seven extra E3/E4 episodes can increase the count. Define base-cell versus reserve accounting and sampling weights. A 512-update checkpoint cadence cannot prove uninterrupted coverage during writes; either instrument commits/writes or narrow the assertion. Run at least one complete 144-config refresh cycle, with all intended E4 variants and E3 load bins exercised, before claiming refresh-wide stability.

6. **Prevent a low velocity score from becoming a false holding claim.** Per-scene RMSE matches the brief's numerical criterion, but aggregation across configs can hide large local failures. A single 3 rad/s configuration among 143 perfect ones contributes only 3/√144 = 0.25 rad/s to an equally weighted scene aggregate. Preserve the specified aggregate metric, report every config, and pre-register a per-config guard if the claim is all-config competence. Require target-position and unintended-stop/contact checks for a stronger equilibrium-holding claim: a joint immobilized at the wrong angle can pass the velocity gate. This is a limitation of the claim, not an assertion that the brief already mandated a position threshold.

7. **Make controls and budget outcomes decisive.** A rule-control failure is a failed validation, not a license to declare the task physically impossible. Specify outcomes if the 12-hour cap arrives before the first scheduled evaluation, and include evaluation/collection time in the cap. Missing metrics and mixed seeds are NOT PASSED. Budget exhaustion does not refute asymptotic H2, but it does fail a claim of closure within the registered budget. Keep coverage_ready and holding PASS separate and require both for an overall B02 success.

8. **Tighten the mechanism probes.** Define recovery time and censoring when only one saturation arm recovers; both arms need identical smoothing unless smoothing is the treatment. S3's −AS removes both penalty and smoothing, so it identifies the combined intervention, not the penalty alone. Freezing action-history channels in C-B02-A-03 disrupts feedback and observability as well as any integrating role. The proposed 3× and R² criteria are an exploratory signature, not proof of a necessary internal model. Include matched perturbation controls and avoid inferring necessity from a single-seed ablation.

Acceptance should require a corrected specification followed by the registered physical and memory results. The current document supports a promising design direction and a valid narrow capacity bound; it does not yet establish the claimed 720-cell readiness guarantee or resolution of all six audit issues.
