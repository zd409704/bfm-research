## 2. Rubric-Keyed Technical Objections

**Exactness of Serial-Chain Coupling Reduction — 30%.**

**D1 [CRITICAL] The X/S partition mistakes an off-diagonal inertial term for an exogenous disturbance (§1.4, L3).** The displayed mass matrix and velocity/gravity terms are consistent with the stated idealized rigid-body model. The ensuing causal interpretation is not. With contact absent,

\[
\ddot Q=M^{-1}(B\tau-C-G),\qquad
\frac{\partial\ddot Q_k}{\partial\tau_j}=(M^{-1}B)_{kj}.
\]

Off-diagonal entries generally do not vanish. A knee torque changes hip and base acceleration during the same physical interval, even if the hip command is held fixed. Contact introduces further coupled dependence. Calling `-M_jk Qddot_k` “other unit / base only (one-step causal)” does not establish action independence. Holding another controller's command fixed is different from prescribing its acceleration with an external apparatus. Consequently, the X+K amplitude and X bandwidth quantities do not have the causal interpretation claimed, and the assertion that only the diagonal self term changes the local plant is misleading. Either retain the full coupled system and analyze its input-output response, or explicitly define an intervention under which a housing trajectory is prescribed. Do not infer exogeneity from row location in the mass matrix.

**D2 [MAJOR] `D-ASSIGN-1` assigns two incompatible nominal hip inertias (§1.3).** A thigh-only descriptor with the printed single-unit inertia convention gives

\[
J^{\mathrm{tr}}_1=I_1+m_1l_{c1}^2+n^2J_r=0.020675\;\mathrm{kg\,m^2}.
\]

The proposal instead sets it equal to the full-chain `M11(q2=1)`, which evaluates to approximately `0.05000056 kg m²`, or 2.418 times the thigh-only value. If the shank is entirely a distal load, its inertia cannot simultaneously be hidden inside the training reference inertia. If the training descriptor independently carries a larger inertia, specify that value and demonstrate a matching training configuration rather than asserting the equality follows from the thigh-only choice. This changes the hip self term, inertia ratios, hull membership, and envelope table. The algebraic rearrangement remains an identity for either reference value, so an identity-residual test will not catch this semantic mismatch.

**D3 [MAJOR] The stance projection is not yet a per-actuator effective inertia (§1.5–1.6).** For a sticking-foot manifold `Q=Q(s)`, let `v=dQ/ds`. Then the scalar inertia is `vᵀMv`, but the actuator input is `v_qᵀτ`. Rescaling `s` changes that scalar inertia. A ratio to a particular unit's `Jtr` needs a fixed normalization, such as `s=q2`, plus an explicit treatment of the other actuator's torque/feedback. For the equal-length, foot-under-hip branch, `dq1/dq2=-1/2`; the reduced input is therefore `τ2-τ1/2`, not `τ2` alone. A contact-free response with other coordinates allowed to move also need not have effective inertia `Mjj`; eliminating free coordinates produces a different response. The proposed switch between `Mjj` in flight and an unspecified projection in stance does not define a comparable stability certificate. Finite-stiffness penalty contact is also not an exact pinned manifold: define how compliance, slip, and transition intervals are handled. Publish the normalization, reduced dynamics, and physical meaning of each reported ratio.

**D4 [MAJOR] The static “infeasible” cell depends on an arbitrary internal-force allocation.** The 3 kg squat calculation fixes `λx=0`, although §1.4 explicitly establishes a family of static equilibria indexed by horizontal contact force. Using the printed model at `q1=-0.6`, `q2=1.2`, and ignoring the omitted friction as the proposal does, I obtain commands approximately `(-0.05970,-0.96473)` at `λx=0`. Taking `λx=-3 N` instead gives `(0.05035,-0.88220)`, with `λz=52.4835 N` and friction bound `μλz=41.9868 N`. Thus this same static posture admits both commands below 0.92 under the stated contact cone. Moreover, 0.92 is a training/readiness ceiling, not the physical action limit of 1. Static infeasibility must be established over all admissible force allocations, not one allocation. Separate actuator impossibility, training-envelope exceedance, and failure of a chosen controller. This check does not certify the dynamic squat trajectory; it disproves the stated static basis for excluding the configuration.

**D5 [MAJOR] Several coverage conclusions exceed the supplied dynamics.**

- In flight, the first row gives `M_yy(yddot+g)=-M_yq qddot-C_y`. The system COM is ballistic; the pelvis need not be. Hence `yddot≈-g` and the claimed complete gravity-load release require a quantified approximation during joint motion.
- `r(f)=|1-exp(-i2πfτd)|` is the residual of a particular delayed cancellation signal. It is not a lower bound for every causal observer or coordinator: predictable periodic inputs can be anticipated, and the upper layer sees both histories and the reference. It also does not directly predict position error without the closed-loop transfer function.
- E4 is described here as load release plus quiet recovery, yet landing is conditionally admitted by an unspecified E4 impulse bound. A release and an impact are different transition laws. The certificate must check the actual trained E2/E4 contact/release mechanisms, including stiffness, damping, onset, duration, and state dependence; impulse magnitude alone is insufficient. The listed E2 stiffness range never appears in the certificate.
- Large individual contact torque does not by itself imply a stop collision or a particular sign of joint work: inertia and the other coupled terms enter the balance. Nor do separate component maxima establish a bound on their signed sum. Qualify these assertions and report total required torque and work/velocity diagnostics.

The knee housing-transport identity is a valuable instantaneous rigid-body identity under its stated assumptions. It does not by itself prove coverage equivalence of trajectory distributions, an unavoidable observation delay, or the flight approximation.

**Fairness of Modular vs Raw-Torque Ablation — 25%.**

**F1 [MAJOR] The action interfaces leave SAC's optimization problem underspecified (§2.2–2.3).** A samples a 32-dimensional pre-projection variable but executes two 15-dimensional sphere directions. Scaling either nonzero pre-projection vector changes its Gaussian density without changing the executed command. If entropy is computed before projection, radial exploration is rewarded despite having no behavioral effect; if it is computed on the sphere, the induced density and critic coordinates must be defined. State which action is stored, which action the critic receives, and which log density/entropy dimension is used. Supply B's position/velocity/acceleration bounds, output transforms, B-latent normalization, and B-blend's exact parameterization. Equal hyperparameter-grid size does not repair an undefined entropy objective. Kinematic completion ensures algebraic consistency of outcome fields, not dynamic reachability or membership in the encoder's training distribution.

**F2 [MAJOR] Parameter and preprocessing controls are incomplete.** No `P_A`, A width, critic architecture, or count convention is fixed. The rule “smallest width matching A” is circular until A is specified, and matching actor and critic separately may need separate widths. S-G5 requires all seven arms within 1%, while C-big is deliberately `P_A+281,617`. Explicitly exempt and separately audit that arm. State whether the common normalizer is fitted once from a common calibration dataset or separately from each arm's trajectories; “identical running normalizer” is otherwise ambiguous. Preserve the frozen local actor's original input preprocessing. C-PD's bias/anti-windup state must use only information available under the shared observation contract, rather than simulator payload/contact fields.

**F3 [MAJOR] Some representational conclusions are stronger than the controls establish (§2.4, §3.5).** A raw network with sufficient capacity can approximate the composite policy; that does not establish inclusion in the particular parameter-matched C class. C-big is useful but does not guarantee it either. A-rand may differ radically in torque range, sensitivity, and conditioning, so A beating A-rand alone cannot identify the source of the gain. Record those action-map diagnostics and interpret C-PD alongside it. Failure to reject a performance difference is not equivalence; the proposed tie-band rule needs an equivalence interval contained in a prespecified practical margin. The packet's 5-point adjudication tie margin also should not be confused with a separately chosen 10% sample-efficiency margin.

**Morphology-Transfer Falsifiability — 25%.**

**T1 [CRITICAL] Primary eligibility is conditioned on outcomes of the controller being tested (§1.6, §3.5).** A-NULL declares any slip or sufficiently large internal-force drift OUT; the remaining diagnostics also use visited trajectories. Those quantities depend on arm, seed, and training progress. A modular controller that produces unstable force or slips can thereby remove its failure from the H4 comparison. The proposal never states whose trajectories define the common cell label. This is outcome-dependent selection, not a fixed coverage proviso. Define shared eligibility before primary learning, using fixed independent probe trajectories/controller, training-support evidence, and explicit treatment of uncertainty. Retain later instability/slip as outcomes. Report all-cell results as well as the fixed eligible subset.

There is also no demonstrated path to the “≥2 of 3 tasks” decision: nominal T2 is predicted OUT by A-RHO, T3 is OUT or unresolved by A-IMP, and bandwidth/hull bounds are missing. Establish eligible task cells in advance or declare the primary H4 test unavailable. Do not count an empty eligible set as support or falsification.

**T2 [CRITICAL] The retention metric reverses the direction of improvement for the printed rewards (§3.5, P3).** T1/T2 returns are negative costs. If nominal return is `-10` and transfer worsens to `-20`, the proposed `Rcell/Rnom` rises to 2; improvement to `-5` lowers it to 0.5. P3 rewards the former. Ratios also become unstable near zero. Use task success/error endpoints or a preregistered positive skill normalization with fixed reference anchors. Define the normalized-return AUC and the 5% non-inferiority margin on that same interpretable scale. Recompute the P3 hypotheses accordingly.

**T3 [MAJOR] The morphology intervention is not reproducible or isolated (§3.1, §3.3–3.4).** Nominal hip `C0=9` becomes `C0=6` in the transfer row. This silently adds a hip-capacity intervention to supposed shank-length/knee-capacity swaps. Specify separate one-factor cells and explicitly named combined cells. For each new `L2`, define mass, COM, central inertia, foot geometry, stops, friction, and calibration changes. Marginal inclusion in length/capacity ranges does not imply inclusion in the joint seed hull, much less empirical local coverage. Provide the actual mapping and hull decision before claiming the units remain locally in-distribution. Clarify the Cartesian product versus individual swaps and the nominal case against which every transfer endpoint is compared.

**T4 [MAJOR] Censoring and prediction tests do not support the stated interpretation.** Replacing censored success times with 2M can erase differences or move ratios toward unity; it is not generally “conservative against A/B.” Report success probability by budget and a defined censored-time estimand, with resampling at the seed level and paired task keys. Failure to establish P3's advantage is not evidence against it: a lower confidence bound ≤0 is an inconclusive result, not a refutation. P1 says both A and B improve while §3.5 accepts A or B; choose one primary claim and multiplicity rule. Likewise, a nonsignificant P2 correlation cannot refute the mechanism. Define inconclusive outcomes, including EDGE cells, which currently have no clear primary status.

**Benchmark Reproducibility — 20%.**

**R1 [MAJOR] The contact and task definitions do not specify one executable environment (§3.1–3.2).** A normal spring/damper and Coulomb bound do not define tangential force, stick/slip transitions, normal-force clamping, integration, stop forces, or termination/reset behavior. A point-foot Jacobian and a finite-radius foot need a consistent gap/contact convention. Friction seed assignment, calibration distributions, sine amplitudes/phases, reset velocities, squat interpolation, task/cell sampling probabilities, and train/test episode-key construction are missing. “Equivalent” MuJoCo contact needs a quantitative calibration target and tolerance. The base rail adds a passive coordinate and a horizontal reaction; that is a legitimate minimal apparatus if clearly stated, but results cannot be generalized automatically to an unconstrained floating-base limb.

**R2 [MAJOR] Joint geometry does not observe base height in flight.** Under a specified no-slip ground contact, link geometry can infer pelvis height. In flight, the same joint angles admit arbitrary vertical translations; joint-derived “height” is leg extension. Specify simulator-truth height, foot clearance, apex and landing events for evaluator/reward computation, while keeping them out of policy inputs. Do not score airborne stabilization using only joint-derived height. The five-second LAND-HOP schedule, mixture of drop and hop episodes, first-contact timing, and success aggregation over 50 evaluations also need definitions.

**R3 [MINOR] Several claims and counts require narrower wording or correction.** `7×3×10` is 210 runs and 420 million training steps at 2M each, not approximately 420 runs. Running both R-NOM and R-MULTI would yield 420 base runs and 840 million steps; say whether both are required. A 4096-sample latent torque range is a sampled lower bound on reachability, not a proof of an asymptotic ceiling. A finite nearest-neighbor threshold need not give zero measured off-image rate even for true encoder outputs; exclude self-matches when calibrating it. Name the absent bandwidth/impulse/hull artifacts and their freeze points rather than calling the specification complete.

## 3. Audit of Rejected Alternative & Necessity Claim

**Rejected E5 moving-housing/IMU alternative.** Rejecting the proposed 140-D actor and retraining for the primary B04 experiment is justified by the fixed deployment schema and frozen-reuse task. These are sufficient scope reasons. The stronger scientific rejection is not established:

- Housing acceleration is action-dependent in the freely moving chain, so sensing it does not convert the coupling into an exogenous signal. Measurement and filtering also prevent an automatic zero-delay cancellation guarantee.
- Extra motion measurements and moving-housing training could help identify effective dynamics; the assertion that they give “no information” about reflected inertia is too strong. They do not directly measure payload or internal force, and the transport identity alone proves neither success nor impossibility for S/K effects.
- Giving the same additional measurement to C would define a different, potentially fair later study. It is not inherently an unfair ablation once both arms receive it.
- An in-contract alternative deserves consideration: the upper layer already sees both local histories and can generate coordinated latent/outcome commands, possibly using a model-based feasibility projection. That preserves the frozen local inputs and weights. The rejection of computed-torque changes *inside* the local actor does not reject coordination *above* it.

The contingency should be triggered by a defined diagnostic study, including OUT cells. Requiring H4 falsification on IN cells while attributing the gap to out-of-envelope bandwidth can screen out the very cases needed to motivate E5.

**`C-B04-A-01`: retain the narrow warning, reject the claimed necessity proof as written.**

**[MAJOR] Part (a) confuses intervention laws with trajectory support.** An open-loop generator cannot reproduce an action-responsive torque law under arbitrary interventions, but it can produce the same torque trajectory along a particular realized action/state path. The identically zero self torque at zero acceleration is an immediate overlap example. Broad open-loop path distributions can also assign support to trajectories generated by a feedback law. Conditional independence is not disjointness of support. Conditioning on a fully specified generator state can even make the stated independence trivial because the torque is deterministic. State the causal property as invariance under interventions on local actions and specify whether support means marginal torque paths or joint counterfactual laws. D1 further invalidates treating the remaining off-diagonal acceleration terms as automatically exogenous.

**[MAJOR] Part (b) supports a local warning, not the stated general amplitude certificate failure.** A delayed PD loop can lose stability after an inertia change, and unstable linear motion can start with arbitrarily small amplitude. This establishes that small *onset* torque does not certify stability. It does not show that the torque remains inside an amplitude envelope for the entire infinite trajectory. On a finite horizon one can scale the initial condition sufficiently small, but that horizon and criterion must be stated. The exact discrete plant, zero-order hold, delay convention, controller recurrence, nominal inertia, and saturation treatment are absent, so the spectral radius and threshold are not reproducible from “50 Hz PD, 2 Hz, ζ=0.7.” An isolated servo at `ρ=0.25` also does not construct the asserted chain trajectory: show how that local response is realized by the specified coupled system. The reported stance issue is mainly large inertia, whereas the instability example uses reduced inertia.

**[MAJOR] Part (c) does not follow.** The counterexample can establish insufficiency of an amplitude-only guarantee over a specified family of plants. It does not prove that *every* valid certificate must contain these particular A-RHO and A-NULL thresholds. A direct robust-stability, dissipativity, or full coupled response certificate could account for the same physics without those coordinates. Training-hull membership is neither demonstrated sufficient nor necessary for control stability. The conditional PI example requires actual integral dynamics and inconsistent targets; neither follows from frozen actor weights or a previous-torque input. Therefore it cannot establish a universal stance-null-mode requirement for all frozen actors.

The stated AUC “falsifier” explicitly does not falsify the theorem. It tests incremental predictive usefulness for one checkpoint and dataset. Separate the mathematical claim from that empirical diagnostic, specify held-out cell evaluation and uncertainty, and avoid using performance-dependent eligibility in either predictor.

**Acceptable replacement claim:** Within a precisely defined sampled controller/plant family, matching a scalar disturbance-amplitude bound alone does not guarantee closed-loop stability after composition; the effective coupled dynamics and contact constraints must also be considered. Prove that limited statement independently. Present A-RHO/A-NULL as proposed diagnostics pending validation, not logically necessary certificate components. The requested Grade 1 for the current bundled claim is unsupported.

**Related `C-B04-A-03` qualification.** In a fixed linearized tangent expressed in deviations from equilibrium, `w=φ2 I1-φ1 I2` has `wdot=φ2 q1*-φ1 q2*`. The force-driving quantity `ki w`, not `w` itself, has the extra `ki` in its growth rate and leaky bound. The physical force mode also needs the actuator scaling and coordinate mapping. State these conventions and the local fixed-tangent approximation. A pair of learned actors with anti-windup-like behavior need not realize ideal integrators. Small measured force drift does not uniquely prove the absence of integral action: leakage, saturation handling, target reachability, or coordination can also suppress it.

## 4. Spike Protocol Critique

The spike appropriately avoids claiming to test H4 with untrained units. Its present gates mix algebraic consistency, illustrative mechanisms, architecture instantiation, and closed-loop simulation, and several cannot validate the claims assigned to them.

| Gate | Critique and required revision |
|---|---|
| **S-G1 — reduction** | Comparing an EOM row with its own rearrangement can pass with the same erroneous model on both sides. Independently derive kinetic/potential energy or use an independently implemented rigid-body model; check energy consistency, gravity gradients, and rotor contributions. Add action perturbations demonstrating off-diagonal acceleration response, plus prescribed-housing tests for the transport identity. Check descriptors separately: residual arithmetic cannot detect D2. Define all sampling ranges and morphology parameters. |
| **S-G2 — envelope** | Specify which quantities must be monotone. The proposal's own hip required-command maximum falls from 0.075 at 0.5 Hz to 0.049 at 1 Hz; gravity/inertia cancellation makes blanket frequency monotonicity inappropriate. Squat torque crossing 0.92 at `λx=0` must not be treated as physical infeasibility. Reproduce that explicitly constrained calculation separately from a feasible-contact-force optimization. Freeze numeric manifests and distinguish component maxima, net torque, and impulse windows. Three identical reruns show determinism, not physical correctness. |
| **S-G3 — delayed PD** | Publish the discrete state-transition matrix and controller recurrence, including delay indexing, hold/integration convention, initial delayed state, nominal inertia/capacity, and saturation. Compare eigenvalues with an independent rollout over several initial scales. Specify what “spectral radius matches simulation” means for oscillatory modes. Distinguish infinitesimal instability, finite-horizon amplitude bounds, and bounded saturated behavior. Realize the relevant effective dynamics in a chain before claiming a chain counterexample. Failure of the quoted numbers requires correction or withdrawal of that construction, not automatic preservation by a grade downgrade. |
| **S-G4 — null mode** | Supply the tangent normalization, equilibrium origin, gains, force mapping, and recurrence. Testing the same scalar integrator formula numerically is weak evidence for the physical effect. Add a constrained mechanical rollout with consistent/inconsistent targets and explicit anti-windup/leakage controls. Compare internal force as well as `w`. Check the intended sticking regime and finite contact compliance. A successful PI surrogate demonstrates a conditional mechanism, not that the learned actor has that mechanism. |
| **S-G5 — fairness** | Fix architecture sizes and input preprocessing first. Exempt C-big from the equal-trainable-count rule and check its intended count separately. Audit optimizer membership and mutable state in addition to hashes. Add action-density/replay conventions and provenance checks showing that no privileged field enters observations, normalization, servo bias, or action decoding. Equal dimensions alone do not establish equal information. |
| **S-G6 — evaluator controls** | Provide the full environment, controller, resets, and episode keys. A C-PD failure can indicate bad gains, contact implementation, or an infeasible reference, not necessarily an evaluator bug. Random fixed latents are not guaranteed to fail all sampled trials, so an observed success should be inspected rather than automatically declaring a bug. Use deterministic success/failure fixtures to validate evaluator logic, then treat stochastic controller success rates as separate integration evidence. Include T3 flight/apex/landing fixtures; T1/T2 controls leave the most consequential evaluator ambiguity untested. |

**[MAJOR] The “CPU, numpy, ≤5 min” scope is not substantiated.** Network/critic instantiation, seven arm interfaces, and contact rollouts require more implementation than the described algebraic calculations. Supply runnable dependencies and a measured runtime, or split the algebraic spike from the environment/learner integration spike. Do not omit the latter while reporting the combined spike as passed.

**[MAJOR] Main-study predictions need a fixed analysis plan before execution.** In addition to repairing the primary eligibility and retention endpoints, freeze the eligible-cell panel, missing-manifest policy, evaluation aggregation, normalizations, censored-time estimand, seed pairing, and multiplicity family. Reconcile the A-or-B support rule with P1's A-and-B prediction. P5 must allow C-PD not having the largest force drift as a scientific result; anti-windup or good target coordination can explain it, so that ranking is not an evaluator invariant. Distinguish evidence against a quantitative prediction from insufficient precision. Treat the CPU 1M fallback as a separately specified endpoint, rather than assuming it is equivalent to the 2M study.

**Release disposition.** The minimal repair is to correct the causal/descriptor reduction, define contact and transfer dynamics completely, establish a controller-independent eligible panel, replace signed-return retention, and make the spike executable with independent checks. Only then can a passing spike justify benchmark release. H4 support or falsification still requires the real 720-cell-ready frozen unit and the preregistered downstream experiments.
