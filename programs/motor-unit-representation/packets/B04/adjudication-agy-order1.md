### Confounds Paragraph

Three structural confounds governed this adjudication and must be controlled during execution:
1. **Verification Depth vs. Specification Granularity**: Candidate P executed author-side numerical verification scripts (`coupling_envelope.py`, `inertia_ratio.py`) on its chosen reference plant (`LIMB2-REF`), ensuring that its task postures and pelvis-mounted payloads were physically feasible and spanned the E3/E4 regimes, whereas Candidate Q wrote a more detailed numerical integrator and parameter-mask specification on paper without evaluating static stance torque balance at its chosen $0.40\text{ m}$ link lengths and $3.70\text{ kg}$ total mass—inadvertently rendering two of its three nominal tasks and 8 of its 9 transfer cells physically impossible ($|u_2| \in [0.97, 1.81]$) and placing its squat payload on the grounded foot where static joint torque impact is identically zero.
2. **Action-Space Geometry vs. Representational Prior**: Comparing a 32-D radial-projection latent interface (Mode A), a desired-outcome interface (Mode B), and a 2-D $\tanh$ torque interface (Baseline C) under standard RL algorithms (SAC in P, PPO in Q) inherently confounds the learned motor representation $(B_\omega, \pi_\theta)$ with exploration geometry (spherical SNR and radial entropy invariance in Mode A) and adapter smoothness (closed-form $\mathcal{K}_c$ in P vs. discontinuous 33-start SLSQP $P_\kappa$ in Q). Only by combining P's smooth $\mathcal{K}_c$ completion and `A-rand`/`C-PD`/`C-big` control arms with surface-density-corrected spherical exploration can downstream sample-efficiency gains be attributed to the frozen representation rather than action-head reparameterization.
3. **Endogenous Stability Failures vs. Exogenous Out-of-Distribution Filtering**: In a coupled closed-loop kinematic chain, trajectory-level quantities such as reflected stance inertia $\rho_j(t)$, internal horizontal force drift $|\dot{\lambda}_x|$, and foot slip depend on the controller's own actions. Conditioning cell eligibility (`IN` vs. `OUT`) on evaluation rollouts of the learned controllers (as in P's `A-NULL` exclusion) creates survivorship bias that can screen out modular control instabilities, whereas setting an overly fine 6,250-bin joint support gate at $N=20$ (as in Q's Stage 1) creates statistical false rejections that force `INCONCLUSIVE` outcomes. Freezing cell eligibility on controller-independent reference probes prior to Stage 2 training eliminates both biases.
