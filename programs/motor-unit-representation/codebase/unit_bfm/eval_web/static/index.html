Training scope: V1 / V2 are all x-z flats, not just a projection of 3D tracks

MuJoCo itself is a three-dimensional simulator, but all of the struts around the y-axis are currently in motion; the root of the activity is only x, z-ziding and pitch. Training is also limited to flat motion and Fx, Fz, My. The current weights can only be tested for partial control under flat conditions, and cannot prove a three-dimensional adaptation to kinetics. Individual electric machines can still be trained in the roots of the three-dimensional movement and in the adjacent poles; this requires an additional extension of authentic freedom, observation, B-result characteristics and sampling.
